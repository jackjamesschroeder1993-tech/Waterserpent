Blocked Septic Tank & Treatment Plant Repairs Rural & Outer-East Specialists

In the outer eastern and Yarra Valley areas where mains sewer does not reach, a working septic tank or domestic wastewater treatment plant is non-negotiable. Water Serpent Plumbing services, unblocks and repairs septic systems and HSTPs across the Dandenongs, Yarra Ranges and Knox region, with same-week response for blockages and overflows.

⚠️ Septic Warning Signs

Warning sign What it usually means
🚽 Gurgling after every flushA partial blockage building in the house-to-tank line
💧 Effluent surfacing in the yardBlocked or failed line — this needs attention now
👃 Smells near the tank or gullyBlockage, full tank or a venting problem
🐢 Whole house draining slowlyThe line to the tank is nearly closed — act before overflow

We diagnose the cause — whether it is a blocked inlet, a failed pump, a soak-trench failure or a tank that simply needs pumping out — and work with liquid-waste contractors where pump-outs are required.

Common Warning Signs

🐢 Slow-draining house with no mains sewer

Classic sign the septic inlet or tank is overloaded.

💧 Wet patches over the trenches

Soak-trench failure or hydraulic overload.

👃 Sewage smell in the yard

Breached lid, blocked vent or surface breakout.

🚨 HSTP alarms

Pump failure, blower failure, or float-switch fault.

⚠️ Effluent surcharging from the inspection opening

Tank or downstream line completely blocked.

Our Diagnostic & Repair Process

1️⃣ System inspection

We identify your system type (septic, HSTP, aerated), confirm last pump-out date and assess load.

2️⃣ Inlet & outlet check

We inspect inlet baffles, outlet tees and any inspection openings.

3️⃣ CCTV on house drain

We confirm the issue is not actually in the line between the house and the tank.

4️⃣ Tank & soak-trench advice

We recommend pump-out, blower service, soak-trench renewal or system upgrade as needed.

5️⃣ Contractor coordination

We manage the liquid-waste contractor on your behalf for tank pump-outs.
