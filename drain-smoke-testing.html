Drain Smoke Testing — Find the Smell at the Source

Mystery sewer smells, illegal cross-connections, breached stack vents and unseen pipe defects are exactly what drain smoke testing is built to expose. Water Serpent Plumbing uses non-toxic, odourless professional smoke machines to pressurise your sewer or stormwater system and show — visually — exactly where it is leaking, venting or cross-connected.

👃 What Smoke Testing Solves

Symptom What the smoke usually finds
🚽 Sewer smell in one bathroomA dry floor-waste trap or unsealed pan collar
🌧️ Smells after heavy rainA cracked pipe or vent letting gas escape under pressure
🏠 Whole-house odour that comes and goesA failed vent or hidden pipe defect in walls or subfloor
🐜 Pests appearing near drainsOpenings into the drainage system — smoke shows every gap

Smoke testing is invaluable for owners corporations, real estate vendors, councils and homeowners chasing problems that CCTV alone cannot solve.

Common Warning Signs

👃 Persistent sewer smell with no visible cause

A cracked stack, missing trap or breached vent is leaking sewer gas.

🌧️ Stormwater cross-connected to sewer

A major compliance issue often hidden under decades of renovations.

🏢 Owners-corp odour complaints

Multi-storey buildings need smoke testing to isolate the failing unit.

🚫 Suspected unauthorised connections

Pool overflows, downpipes or laundry tubs incorrectly connected to sewer.

🏡 Pre-purchase peace of mind

Pair with CCTV for a complete picture of drainage health.

Our Diagnostic & Repair Process

1️⃣ System isolation

We cap any open vents that should not see smoke and identify the entry inspection point.

2️⃣ Smoke introduction

Professional electric smoke generators pressurise the system with safe, non-toxic vapour.

3️⃣ Visual survey

We walk the property and roof line documenting every point where smoke emerges, photographing each finding.

4️⃣ Report & recommendations

A written report shows each defect, its location and our recommended repair approach.
