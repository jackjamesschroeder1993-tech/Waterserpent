🚨 Toilet Overflowing? Do These Four Things First

1️⃣ Stop flushing

A second flush is how bathrooms flood. Whatever is down there will not clear itself with more water behind it.

2️⃣ Turn off the cistern tap

The small isolation tap on the wall beside or behind the toilet. Turn it clockwise.

3️⃣ Prop up the float

If the water is still rising, lift the cistern lid and hold the float arm up to stop the refill.

Why Toilets Block in Melbourne's East Specifically

After twenty years clearing drains between Ringwood and the Yarra Valley, the causes here are not the national average. Three local factors do most of the damage:

🌳 Mature gums over old earthenware

Homes through Ferntree Gully, Croydon, Montrose and the Ranges sit on clay pipe with rubber-ring or mortar joints, surrounded by thirsty natives. Roots find the joint, then thicken. The toilet backs up, but the toilet was never the problem.

💧 4.5-litre pans on old fall

Modern water-efficient toilets flush with a fraction of the water the drain was designed around. On a line that already has a slight belly or a partial root mass, there isn't enough volume to carry waste through. New toilet, old pipe — blocks constantly.

🧻 "Flushable" wipes

They don't break down. They bind into a rope, snag on anything rough inside the pipe and collect everything that follows. I pull them out somewhere in Melbourne's east most weeks. Bin them — see why flushable wipes block drains.

Less common but worth knowing: sagging or offset pipes in pre-1980s homes, a blocked vent that stops the drain breathing (the cause of most mystery gurgling), and on unsewered properties out through the Ranges, a full or blocked septic system rather than a drain fault at all.

Renting? Here's Who Actually Pays

This comes up on about one call in five, and most renters don't know where they stand. In Victoria, under the Residential Tenancies Act 1997, a blocked or broken toilet is classified as an urgent repair where it's the only toilet at the property — which means it's the rental provider's (landlord's) responsibility to fix, and to fix quickly.

📋 The short version for renters: Tell your rental provider or agent immediately, in writing. If they can't be reached or don't act, you're entitled to arrange the urgent repair yourself with a licensed plumber — up to $2,500 — then give written notice of the work and cost within 7 days. They must reimburse you within 7 days of that notice. The exception is when the renter caused the blockage (wipes, nappies, a toy down the pan), in which case the cost usually falls to the renter.

We work with renters, landlords, agents and body corporates across Melbourne's east every week. We invoice whoever you nominate, itemise the work plainly, and if the CCTV shows the cause was roots or a structural pipe fault rather than anything the tenant did, that's on the footage and in the report — which settles the argument before it starts. See our property manager and commercial drain service for ongoing arrangements.

This is general information, not legal advice — for your specific situation check with Consumer Affairs Victoria or Tenants Victoria.

Warning Signs It's About to Get Worse

🌊 Water rises on flush

The pan fills instead of emptying — pan or trap blockage.

🐌 Slow, lazy flush

A partial blockage downstream. Cheapest moment to fix it is now.

🫧 Gurgling elsewhere

Air being pulled through water seals — a venting or main-line problem, not the toilet.

👃 Sewer smell

A dry or damaged vent, or escaping gas. Smoke testing finds the exact point.

🌱 Wet, green patch in the yard

Waste escaping a cracked line underground. Needs a camera, not a plunger.

🔁 Blocked more than once this year

You're paying repeatedly to clear a symptom. One camera run ends it.

How We Clear It — Our Process

1️⃣ Find where it is

We check the outside inspection opening first. Clear there means the blockage is between it and the pan; backed up means it's further down. That one check decides the whole job.

2️⃣ Pan-safe clearing

Coated closet augers and bowl-safe equipment that will not chip, scratch or crack the ceramic. We're insured either way.

3️⃣ Camera if it's main-line

More than one fixture affected, or a blockage that's returned? We run the CCTV camera and you watch the screen with us.

4️⃣ Jet or eel to suit

Matched to pipe diameter and blockage type — a mini-jetter on a branch line, 5000 PSI on a root-bound sewer.

5️⃣ Test, clean, report

Flush-tested in front of you, work area cleaned properly, written invoice. If we found a structural fault you get the footage.

6️⃣ Fix the cause, if there is one

Roots through a cracked joint get quoted for relining — no-dig, after you've seen why it's needed.
