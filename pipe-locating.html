Underground Pipe Detection — No Guesswork, No Damage

Digging blind costs money. Hitting a live drain costs more. Our pipe locating service uses professional-grade electronic detection to trace the exact route and depth of underground drains, sewer lines, stormwater pipes and utilities before any excavation begins.

Situation Why locating first
🏗️ Before digging, fencing or landscapingKnow exactly what’s below before machinery finds it for you

🏠 Older homes with unknown pipe runsOriginal plans rarely survive — the sonde finds the truth

We locate pipes on residential properties, commercial sites and new development blocks across Melbourne's South East — Ringwood, Knox, Maroondah, Yarra Valley, Boronia, Bayswater, Lilydale and surrounds.

When Do You Need Pipe Locating?

⛏️ Before excavation or landscaping

🔩 Before drilling or boring

Confirm underground clearances before any horizontal directional drilling.

🏗️ New builds and extensions

Map all existing services so builders and engineers can design around them.

📷 Investigating blocked or damaged pipes

📐 Easements and boundary disputes

Confirm pipe positions for council submissions or legal records.

Equipment We Use

📡 Electronic Sonde Transmitter

Inserted through the drain and pushed with a camera rod, transmitting a signal we track from the surface to map exact route and depth.

Camera and sonde run simultaneously — live video of condition plus a marked surface position in one call-out.
