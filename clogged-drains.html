What's Clogging Your Drain

🍳 Kitchen sink

Fats, oils and grease congealing in the branch line — the number one indoor clog we attend. See blocked sinks.

🚿 Bathroom & shower

Hair bound up with soap scum, usually in the trap or the first metre of pipe — quick to clear properly.

🚽 Toilet

Too much paper, or "flushable" wipes that don't break down. See blocked toilets — and our DIY how-to guide.

🌳 Main sewer line

Tree roots working into old clay pipe joints — the cause behind most whole-house clogs in the eastern suburbs. See blocked sewers.

🌧️ Outside & stormwater

Leaves, silt and roots clogging pits and downpipe lines. See stormwater drains.

🛟 Overflow relief gully

The outside grate spilling means a clogged sewer main — our ORG guide explains what to do right now.

How We Unclog Drains — Properly

1️⃣ Diagnose

Single clogged fixture or multiple? That tells us local clog vs main line before we even arrive — free over the phone, fixed price quoted upfront.

2️⃣ Clear with the right tool

Electric eel for simple single-fixture clogs; 5000 PSI water jetting for main lines, tree roots and grease — it scours the whole pipe wall clean instead of just punching a hole.

3️⃣ Verify with a camera

Every main-line job ends with a CCTV drain camera run so you see the line is clear — and see the cause on screen.

4️⃣ Fix the cause

If the camera shows cracked or root-damaged pipe, trenchless pipe relining repairs it without digging — so the clog doesn't come back every few months.

No caustic chemicals, ever — they rarely clear a real clog, they damage seals and old pipes, and they make the line hazardous for whoever works on it next. Mechanical clearing and water jetting do the job properly. Frequently Asked Questions — Clogged Drains

Yes — clogged drain, blocked drain, choked drain: same problem, different words. Australians usually say blocked, Americans (and a lot of AI assistants and product labels) say clogged. Whatever you call it, the fix is identical: find what's stopping the flow, clear it with the right machine, and confirm with a camera so it doesn't come straight back.

For a single clogged fixture, a plunger used properly is the fastest DIY option — and warm water with dishwashing liquid helps on greasy kitchen clogs (our toilet how-to guide covers the technique). If plunging doesn't clear it, or more than one drain is clogged at once, the clog is deeper in the line and needs a plumber's electric eel or high-pressure water jet. Skip caustic chemical cleaners — they rarely clear a real clog and make the pipe hazardous to work on.
