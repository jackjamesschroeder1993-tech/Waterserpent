The Inspection Melbourne Home Buyers Skip

When buying a home in Melbourne's East — suburbs like Ringwood, Bayswater, Ferntree Gully, Lilydale or Croydon — most buyers commission a standard building and pest inspection. Far fewer commission a CCTV drain inspection, despite the fact that drainage issues can cost $5,000–$30,000 to rectify.

Why Older Homes Are Higher Risk

Homes built before 1985 in Melbourne's South East almost certainly have earthenware drain pipes. These are now 40–60 years old, and many are showing tree root intrusion, cracked joints, partial collapse, or significant corrosion. None of this is visible in a standard building inspection.

What a Pre-Purchase Drain Inspection Covers

  • Full CCTV inspection of all accessible drain lines
  • Identification of tree root intrusion and severity
  • Detection of cracked, broken or collapsed pipe sections
  • Pipe joint condition and displacement
  • Written report suitable for use in contract negotiations

A pre-purchase drain inspection costs a fraction of what drainage repairs cost. If it reveals a problem, use it to negotiate a price reduction or walk away before it's too late.

How Much Does a Pre-Purchase Drain Inspection Cost?

A CCTV drain inspection with Water Serpent costs $300–$400, and that includes the footage and a written report. Compare that with what a bad drain costs to fix: relining a damaged sewer usually runs $3,000–$15,000 (see our pipe relining cost guide). It's one of the cheapest checks you can do on the biggest purchase you'll ever make.

When to Book It

Before you sign, or before auction day. Auctions in Victoria have no cooling-off period, so once the hammer falls the drains are yours, whatever shape they're in. For a private sale, ask your conveyancer about making your offer subject to a drain inspection. We'll need the agent or the owner to give us access to the property, and it's worth booking a few days ahead so the report is in your hands before you commit.

What the Camera Usually Finds

  • Tree roots growing in through the pipe joints
  • Cracked or shifted clay pipes
  • Sags in the line that hold water and waste
  • Collapsed sections
  • Rough connections left behind by old renovations

Some areas are far more prone to it than others. We ranked them in the Melbourne suburbs where tree roots block the most drains.

What to Do With the Report

If the drains are clean, you buy with confidence. If they're not, you have options: negotiate the price down by the cost of the repair, ask the seller to fix it before settlement, budget for the work yourself, or walk away. The footage and written report give you something solid to put in front of the agent.

Already Bought? You Still Have Options

It's still worth getting the camera through, because knowing beats guessing. A small root problem caught early is a simple clean. Left for a few years, it can become a collapsed pipe. It also pays to check what your policy covers, because many insurers treat tree roots and worn-out pipes differently from sudden damage. We explain it in does home insurance cover blocked drains?

Common Questions

Does a building and pest inspection include the drains?

Usually not. A standard building and pest inspection looks at what the inspector can see. The sewer and stormwater pipes are underground, so they need a separate CCTV drain inspection.

How long does a pre-purchase drain inspection take?

Most residential CCTV inspections take 30 to 60 minutes once Jack is on site. You'll usually get a verbal summary on the day, with the written report and footage to follow.

How much does a pre-purchase drain inspection cost in Melbourne?

With Water Serpent it's $300–$400, including the camera footage and a written report.

Do I need the seller's permission?

Yes. You don't own the property yet, so the agent or the owner needs to give us access. Most agents are used to arranging it alongside the building and pest inspection.

Which homes most need one?

Homes built before the mid-1980s, which usually have clay pipes, and any block with big established trees near the sewer line. Recently renovated homes are worth checking too.
