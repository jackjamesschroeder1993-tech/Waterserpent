Blocked Kitchen & Laundry Sink Repairs Fast, Clean, Guaranteed

Blocked kitchen and laundry sinks are one of the most common call-outs we attend. The vast majority are caused by fats, oils and grease (FOG) congealing inside the trap or the branch waste line. Water Serpent Plumbing clears blocked sinks the same day, with mini-jetters that scour the pipe wall back to bare PVC or copper.

Common Warning Signs

💧 Standing water in the bowl

Classic sign of a trap or branch-line blockage.

🐌 Slow draining after dishwashing

Partial fat build-up restricting flow.

🫧 Gurgling when emptying the bowl

Air being pulled past a partial blockage or a poorly-vented branch.

🤢 Foul smell from the plughole

Biofilm in the trap or dry trap allowing sewer gas back in.

🧺 Laundry trough overflow when the washing machine drains

Gully or main-line blockage downstream.

Our Diagnostic & Repair Process

1️⃣ Diagnosis

We check whether the issue is local to the trap or further down the waste line.

2️⃣ Trap inspection & clean

In many cases the bottle or P-trap is the issue and we clean it on the spot.

3️⃣ Mini-jet or eel the branch

We clear the branch waste line to where it joins the gully or stack.

4️⃣ Hot-water flush

Final flush with hot water and degreaser to leave the line scoured.

5️⃣ Prevention advice

We leave you with practical tips to keep fats and food scraps out of the line.

A slowly draining kitchen sink almost always means fat and food residue have narrowed the waste pipe — each wash coats the pipe wall until only a trickle gets through. If other fixtures are slow too, the problem is further down the main line. A slow drain is the cheap time to fix it: clearing a narrowing pipe is quicker than clearing a fully blocked one that's backed up on a Sunday night.
