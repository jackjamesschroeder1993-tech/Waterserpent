What Causes Blocked Drains in Melbourne?

Melbourne's older eastern and outer-eastern suburbs share a particular mix of problems. Mature gums, liquidambars and other thirsty trees push roots into ageing earthenware and clay pipes, while heavy clay soil shifts through the wet–dry seasons and opens up cracks and joints over time. The causes we clear most often are:

🌳 Tree roots

The number-one cause across the eastern suburbs and Yarra Ranges — roots find a hairline crack and grow into a solid mass that traps everything behind it.

🍳 Grease, fat and food waste

Kitchen build-up that gradually narrows the pipe until flow stops completely.

🧱 Collapsed or misaligned pipes

Common in homes still running their original mid-20th-century earthenware drainage.

🧻 Foreign objects and wipes

So-called "flushable" wipes, sanitary products and kids' toys that snag and build up over time.

🌧️ Stormwater debris

Leaves, silt and sediment that overwhelm pits and downpipes, especially through Melbourne's wetter months.

How Much Does It Cost to Clear a Blocked Drain in Melbourne?

How much it costs to clear a blocked drain in Melbourne comes down to three things: the cause of the blockage, where it sits in the line, and the equipment needed to clear it. A blocked sink, basin or single toilet is usually a quick, lower-cost job. A tree-root-filled sewer main or a blocked stormwater line that needs a CCTV drain camera and high-pressure jetting costs more, simply because it takes more time and gear. Either way, Water Serpent gives you a fixed, upfront price before any work begins — no call-out surprises and no charging by the hour. Common Warning Signs

🚿 Multiple slow drains

Sinks, showers and toilets emptying slowly at the same time almost always means a main sewer or stormwater blockage.

🫧 Gurgling toilets or sinks

Air being forced past a partial blockage as water tries to pass.

👃 Foul smells

Sewer gas escaping through traps that have lost their water seal.

🌊 Water backing up

Water rising in the lowest fixture (often the laundry trough or downstairs toilet) when another is used.

⚠️ Overflowing inspection points or surcharge gullies

A clear sign the main line is fully blocked and requires urgent attention.

Our Diagnostic & Repair Process

1️⃣ On-site assessment

We map your fixtures, identify the affected line and locate the nearest inspection opening.

2️⃣ CCTV camera inspection

We feed a calibrated drain camera into the line to confirm the blockage type and exact location, including depth and distance from the IO. For recurring blockages or pre-purchase checks, see our dedicated CCTV drain inspection service.

3️⃣ Mechanical or hydro-jet clearing

Roots, scale and grease are cut and flushed with the right tool for the pipe material — PVC, earthenware, cast iron or copper.

4️⃣ Post-clear camera verification

We re-film the line so you can see it run free, and we provide footage on request.

5️⃣ Written report & repair options

If we find a structural defect (cracks, offsets, root intrusion) we quote pipe relining or excavation in writing before any further work.
